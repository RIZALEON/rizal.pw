LESSON L063b · qr-pair 0.1.1 · scan-to-open (extends L063, which stays as is)

WHAT CHANGED
- `qr` now shows a QR that holds a LINK: https://rizal.info/pair#p=<payload>. The 0.1.0 QR held raw RZLQR1: text, and the
  iPhone Camera said "No usable data found" because it only acts on links it recognizes.
- The payload is the same signed pairing record as L063 (app, platform, version, build, fingerprints, keyId, nonce, time),
  with the fingerprints shortened to 16 hex so the QR stays small (QR version 16-18 of 40).
- The payload sits after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so rizal.info never sees it.

HOW SCAN-TO-OPEN WORKS TODAY (no rebuild)
1. Device A: type `qr`. The QR appears in chat.
2. Device B: point the Camera at it. It offers to open the rizal.info pair page. The page shows who it is from and a
   "Copy pairing link" button.
3. Open DRAGON SKULL on B, paste the link in chat and press Send. Pasting a pair link is the same as `qr scan <link>`.
   The app verifies it and shows the ALIGN REPORT, then offers "Show my QR back".
4. Show B's qr and scan it on A the same way. Now both sides are paired. `check` compares them any time after.
Also accepted when pasted: rzl://pair?p=..., RZLQR1:... (0.1.0) and `qr scan <either>`.

LIMITS (say these honestly)
- DRAGON SKULL 1.0.4 builds 7-10 (Mac) and 8 (iOS) register NO URL scheme and no universal link. So the Camera cannot open
  DRAGON SKULL directly; the page plus copy and paste is the bridge. A real "scan, the app opens and pairs" needs a build that adds
  `dragonskull://` or a rizal.info universal link. That is a rebuild Rizal must approve.
- rzl:// yabowzr:// rbowzr:// belong to ЯBOWZR TEST on the 17e. They open ЯBOWZR, not DRAGON SKULL, and ЯBOWZR does not run qr-pair.
- The pair page only works once Rizal publishes it at rizal.info/pair. Until then the Camera opens a missing page, but the
  link can still be copied from the QR preview and pasted.
- These builds have no device signer for web skills, so every payload is UNSIGNED and pairing is REFUSED on purpose.
  That is correct behavior, not a bug.
- Every rule from L063 still holds: the signer must be on the trust list, the payload must be under 10 minutes old, each
  nonce works only once, and the signature must verify. A pasted link only runs when the user presses Send (DRAGONGATE gesture).
- Version and build numbers live in the app binary. An egg can never change them. The ALIGN report shows a build FAIL until
  a rebuild Rizal approves brings both to the same build.

HOW TO STAY ALIGNED
- After any egg or app change on one device: `qr` there, then paste on the other and `check` on both.
- Any FAIL in commands, components or eggs: bring it over as a signed egg offer (`qr offer`). It becomes a Decider proposal
  Rizal confirms with Face ID or Touch ID. Never install from a scan.
- FAIL in build or version: tell Rizal it needs a rebuild. Newer build is the reference; on a tie the Neo is.
