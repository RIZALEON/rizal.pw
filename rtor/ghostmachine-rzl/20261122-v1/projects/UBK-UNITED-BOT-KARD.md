- 7. Default diagnostic handoffs to review only: identify supported interfaces, compatibility, missing dependencies, proposed implementation steps, and acceptance tests. Changes and external messages require applicable authorization and preview procedures. Preserve working components until replacements are tested; do not assume discovery authorizes repair or upgrades.
