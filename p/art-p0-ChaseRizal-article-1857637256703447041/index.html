Abstract

The multispecies hominin world of the pleistocene epoch (2.5mya-11kya) and the beginning of the early holocene epoch which began 11kya were both populated by multispecies beings originating from the archipelago of the Philippines and evidence points to the northern island of Luzon as the more specific locale as the point of providence for ISEA(islands of southeast asia or sundaland, the pacific islands, australia, the americas, asia, madagascar, the andaman islands, and beyond. Furthermore the most ancient of these migrations and subsequent civilizations were founded by these progenitor hominins which flourished and thrived in the Philippines due to its natural geomorphological, ecological, and biological manifestations resulting from tectonic, magnetic, hadopelagic, and wallacean abnormalities that created the world's most perfect natural shelter or cradle for evolution, creation, and development of an intellectual species to manifest.

Interestingly enough science and technology have now advanced to such a degree in fields such as paleogenomics, epigenetics, archaeology, and geophysics that we can now synthesize multiple approaches of research to lift the veil on many of our most ancient mysteries the majority of which are found exactly within this epoch, beginning with the pleistocene and entering into the early to mid-holocene. This multimethod synthesis approach was utilized in this research study which I performed utilizing both qualitative and quantitative research from primary and secondary sources. The quantitative based in the most recent scientific peer reviewed studies in areas like paleogenomics such as the groundbreaking research found within Larena et al. 2021. The primary qualitative research is rooted in my ethnographic research in the philippines mainly in situ with many indigenous tribes such as the Ayta Magbukun, Kalinga Butbut, and Ati Boracaynon, although it is also rooted in the earliest ethnographies performed on the peoples of the philippines and most notably those performed by the first Filipino Anthropologists at the turn of the 19th century such as Isabelo de los Reyes and Dr. Jose Rizal. It is this alchemy of science, technology, history and oral traditions of the indigenous peoples of the Philippines that I attempt to reveal a mystery of our shared lost cosmogony.

Armageddon of the Reptilian kings

The end of one world summons the beginning of a new and as that great fiery body descended from the heavens 66 million years ago to disseminate Armageddon for those great reptilian kings, the dinosaurs, A new world of hominids rose from the ashes, our ancient ancestors. This Cosmic trigger manifested volcanic and pyroclastic activity, the likes of which our planet's surface has never experienced. In conjunction with subduction and a great shift of tectonic plates, this sparked the creation of the Philippine islands 65 million years ago from this world’s deepest abyss, the oceans great womb of Mariana.

This antediluvian water world archipelago was virgin soil for new life to create and evolve while, the rest of old world mainland pangea was ravaged by the damage and pestilence caused by the meteor strikes, global tsunami, and raging infernos of hellfire devouring flora and fauna alike. Whichever apex predators survived relentlessly hunted down all remaining life the pangean continents had become the least hospitable location for the evolution and creation of new creatures. Although as our story goes the schematics of our universal design granted beings a vine and fig tree to find solace and respite in a new Eden of the ocean. Pristine and virgin free from the monsters of our past, a golden land, perfectly baked by the sun for a new creation to thrive, evolve, craft and develop into the new world. In this island of the gods the hominids were at the apex and nothing or no one could make them afraid or stand in the path of their destiny.

The Antediluvian Philippines was a multi-species hominid paradise these ancient beings walked, lived and commingled together Homo Luzonensis, Denisovan, Homo Erectus, Neanderthal, Sapien, and others. This convergence, which is cumbersome for certain scholars to address and discourses to take place in mainstream academia, highlights how much more of our story remains in the darkness left to be brought into the light. Science today has a fairly good understanding of the post diluvian world which began around 11,500ya with the Holocene epoch but our antediluvian past which can be demarcated around 2.5 mya at the beginning of the Pleistocene is still very much a mystery. What we do know definitively is that to date the oldest carbon dated biological remains of a hominid ancestor Graecopithicus, was found in the land of the Philippine archipelagos namesake, King Philipp of Macedonia, father of Alexander the Great, Athens greece which dates back over 7 million years ago, This leaves the majority of our great story still left to be told. Thanks to the Science, technological advancement, ethnographic research, and oral traditions that preserved our cosmogony, we are now able to reveal once hidden chapters and bring the codices of our lost past from darkness to light once again.

This prequel to modern history will propose the ideal that before the colonial global circumnavigation of Magallanes whose final destination was the Philippines is ironically enough the place of origin for the pre colonial multispecies seafarers into new uninhabited lands. The Promethean flame did not first blaze a trail from West to East but from East to West starting in the island of Luzon which interestingly enough in the indigenous language translates to the Island or the people of luz or light. The Indigenous Filipino circumnavigated the world leaving traces of its genetics, artifacts, and technologies. The Filipino oral traditions tell us that the old gods walked amongst us—they were our ancestors, our grandfathers and grandmothers. Therefore, we can propose now that these beings evidenced biologically and archaeologically in the Philippines and eventually elsewhere—the Denisovan, Neanderthal, Erectus and Luzonensis were the gods of the ancient world. The question therefore is are the gods dead as mainstream academia proposes? Or do they still walk amongst us in a world filled with secrets and mysteries, could we still live in a multispecies world of gods and men?

A synthesis of the most recent scientific studies relating to genetics, archaeology and paleontology with the ethnographies, histories, myths, and legends of our ancestors, which will allow us a glimpse over the walls that were previously beyond the reach of our limited stature and wandering eyes. The Karakoan Arc

The ancient Filipino were master craftsmen in many fields but one of their most refined was shipbuilding The Karakoa one of their largest had housing areas for crew, giant sails and mounted cannons in precolonial times and could reach speeds between 12 to 15 knots, in fact one of the first spanish chroniclers to arrive in the Philippines stated: “That care and attention, which govern their boat-building, cause their ships to sail like birds, while ours are like lead in this regard." It is this ancient tradition of mastercraft shipbuilding found in the Philippines and throughout ISEA and the Pacific which I believe is not only the modality of global immigration and expansion but a remnant of a progenitor species technology taught to limited initiates and chosen apprentices through rites and ceremonies. This modality of expansion can be evidenced not only through seaferrying but also more concretely now in the most recent paleogenetic studies in which we will reference two main population groups as progenitor populations of the modern world which are the Aeta and the Igorots of the Philippine islands. I will begin with the west to east migrations moving from Luzon into the pacific and beyond.

In a recent genetic study exploring the origins of the eastern polynesian islands It was shown that the most probable match for an origin population was that of the Igorot Kankanaey and lowland Indigenous Filipinos came second in similarity to all four polynesia groups, another interesting aspect of this research was that 97% of all haplogroups found within the polynesian groups of this study were a part of Haplotype group B4a1a1 which originates within the northern tribes of luzon these findings echo a different independent study regarding the peopling of south east asia which also pointed to the Kankanaey Igorots as the closest match to a probable progenitor population for the region out of all global population groups.

Another result of this evidenced that the Kankanaey Igorots geographical isolation and genomics places them as the most probable least-admixed living population group today placing them closest to the source population of ancestors. Interestingly a recent study performed on the topic of mitochondrial population genomics of the peopling of the Americas not only showed direct connections between tribes of the pacific northwest and those of south america but that one of the most shared haplogroups between the 2 is B2 which is one of only 5 haplogroups found in Indigenous americans and shockingly has never been found in siberia-beringia tribes closest to the proposed land bridge, but instead is the haplogroup which is found in its highest frequency and genetically most closely descended from that Haplotype B progenitor population of the Northern indigenous Filipino, further validating the theory I'm postulating which is OPO: The Out of the Philippines theory.

These two living ancestral population groups were initiates of the gods, Nephilim, Titans and so forth of ancient world mythologies or what science now refers to as our ancient hominid ancestors such as the Denisovans who handed down their knowledge, wisdom, genetics and technology to these chosen survivors from the end of the last world and into the new.

During my time with the Ayta Magbukun whom in the most recent Denisovan genetic study showed that they had the highest level of living Denisovan DNA in the world, I learned some very interesting lessons from my ancestors most importantly that there are many mysteries left in this world to be revealed and places left unexplored.
