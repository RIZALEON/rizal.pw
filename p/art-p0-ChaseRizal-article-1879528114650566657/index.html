Island of the Denisovans

@ChaseRizal · 2025-01-15 · article

Monograph: Revenant Hominids

Philippine Aeta possess the highest level of Denisovan ancestry in the world

In the scientific world there are rarely moments when a singularity manifests, shattering the antiquated mechanisms and colonial storylines of academia’s mistaken past assumptions. Although now it seems as such we have arrived at just such a moment and out of respect for all that have paved this road for us to arrive here, a proper scientific definition of a singularity: it is a point where all our known laws and beliefs  become indistinguishable between one another and our space and time become intertwined or commingled and merged in such a way converging to such a degree that they cease to have any independent meaning. This quintessential moment was generated from a brilliant Organismal Biologist at Upssala University in Sweden with this comprehensive and groundbreaking  study performed on Living Indigenous populations in The Philippines.

Since the beginning of our current academic epoch the mainstream theory has been that ancient human ancestors walked their way across the world without appropriate technology, ingenuity, or intent, mainly wandering the wilderness without an aim, hunting and gathering. This study changes that with  highlights such as this: “Denisovan Islanders populated the Philippines prior to the arrival of modern humans.” This expansive and ground breaking first ever Genetic study of Indigenous tribes within the Philippines has shocked the world as it revealed that not only do the Indigenous peoples of the islands have the highest level of living Denisovan DNA out of any existing person or ethnic group in the world. Including the previous group which held that title: the Austraulo-Papuans by at least 30-40%. This is also more profound when taken into account the fact that all indigenous Ayta groups in the Philippines in fact expressed high levels of living genetic neanderthal DNA as well. When taking into account these two dynamics and factoring in finds from recent archaeological digs that uncovered a new ancient hominid ancestor Homo luzonensis dating to at least 70kya on the northernmost island of Luzon forces academia to confront the possibility that the mainstream theory may get turned upside down. We all must confront this question: what were at least 3 different and distinct species of ancient hominids doing 70-500kya on The Islands of the Philippines and how did they get there?

This comprehensive study was performed, collected, and analyzed from 2.3 million genotypes collected with permission from 118 different and distinct Indigenous tribes of The Philippines from many different regions. Interestingly enough the highest levels of Denisovan DNA are found in the Northern most island tribal populations of Luzon which is the northernmost island and was never connected to any other continental land mass to form a land bridge for migration by land bridges as Luzon is a true archipelago and was created by subduction and volcanic activity and is just east of the Philippine Plate and Wallacea/huxley line meaning the ocean depth is so deep that it could have never been connected to any other continent. Leading us to postulate that the “Denisovan Islanders” must have also been Denisovan Seafarers. Another very peculiar finding out of this study is that the only other population group that approaches high levels of living Denisovan DNA are Australopapuans also east of the Wallacea/Huxley line in Oceania.

I am interested deeply in this study as all Anthropologist and academics that are interested in true science should be, we should never be hardlined and firmly concreted in theories that are unsustainable but rather always fluid adaptable and open to new evidence refining our theories and understandings along the way when presented with clear distinct scientific evidence unafraid to acknowledge if our theories were wrong. Not only does this new evidence shatter the current academic timeline for human evolution and migration patterns  in general but it challenges the assumption that our ancient ancestors were much more than just cavemen but instead brilliant explorers with great ingenuity and craftsmanship such as shipbuilding. Although yet and still a parting thought to ponder which is the notion that The Denisovan Islander shipbuilders may have traveled even further as the only other population where this similar Denisovan, Neanderthal, Human admixture is found is in The Indegenous and Native populations of The Americas.
