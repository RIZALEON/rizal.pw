Unraveling the Mystery of Elongated Skulls in the Philippines-University of Wisconsin Madison SEASSI

Presentation: Unraveling the Mystery of Elongated Skulls in the Philippines at University of Wisconsin Madison South East Asian Summer Institute 2023

Title: "Unraveling the Mystery of Elongated Skulls in the Philippines"

Abstract: In "Unraveling the Mystery of Elongated Skulls in the Philippines," the phenomenon of elongated skulls, a shared artifact found globally, investigates various theories surrounding the origins of these skulls, including cultural practices, genetics, ancestral art, and progenitor hominin species, tracing the roots of anthropological research on elongated skulls in the Philippines back to pioneers of anthropology such as Rudolph Virchow, A.B Meyer, and Dr. Jose Rizal. The research uncovers the presence of these skulls in different archaeological sites across the Philippines, from the northern islands of Batanes to the south in Mindanao, dating back thousands of years symbolized beauty, power, nobility, and divine connections in ancient Filipino culture. Amidst the controversies and theories surrounding the presence of elongated skulls, the study proposes the Progenitor Hominid Theory, suggesting that these skulls are puzzle pieces of our shared missing past. The study concludes with the assertion that the Philippines, with its significant amount of living Denisovan and other hominid DNA, holds the key to unlocking the secrets of our shared past, making it a significant contribution to anthropological literature.

OPO: Out of the Philippines Origin Theory

Presentation: The Out of the Philippines Origin (OPO) theory & A multi-species anthropology of the antedeluvian philippines at California Polytechnic University Humboldt

The Out of the Philippines Origin (OPO) theory suggests that the Philippines served as the point of origin for the migration of modern humans. This theory is based on a combination of archaeological, genetic, scientific, and ethnographic evidence. According to the OPO theory, the Philippines, particularly the northern island of Luzon, played a crucial role in the evolution and development of hominids. The unique geomorphological features of the Philippines, including tectonic, magnetic, hadopelagic, and Wallacean abnormalities, created an advantageous environment for hominid evolution. Genetic research supports the OPO theory by indicating that the Philippines is the only location on Earth where high levels of DNA from all direct ancestors of modern humans, including Denisovans, Neanderthals, and Luzonensis, can be found in living populations. Indigenous oral traditions and ethnographic research within the Philippines align with the scientific evidence presented by the OPO theory. These traditions and research provide further support for the idea that the Philippines served as the point of origin for multispecies beings in the Pleistocene epoch and the early Holocene epoch. The OPO theory aims to synthesize various research approaches, including genetics, archaeology, paleogenomics, epigenetics, geophysics, ethnography, and oral traditions, to shed light on the mysteries of our ancient past. By combining both qualitative and quantitative research methods, researchers seek to uncover the lost cosmogony and provide a deeper understanding of human origins. I speak English, Tagalog, and Spanish, I am based in Luzon and am available for research, projects, interviews, and lectures both public and private throughout the Philippines and beyond. Contact me directly on any of my social media for collaborations or contracts.

OPO or the Out of the Philippines Origin Theory, pinpoints the place of providence for our species and the modern world In the Philippines by integrating  multi-method approach integrating science, technology, and anthropology with Indigenous knowledge and histories. I believe the evidence supporting this theory will only grow stronger as the Philippines transitions from the least funded archaeological and anthropological region of the world to a more properly funded and researched nation, thanks enlarge to the multinational community of academics, researchers and anthropologists helping in this proper shift of unbalanced allocations. Within my research I have uncovered substantial evidence for ancient circumnavigations, lost civilizations, and global migrations by the first nations groups of the Philippines such as the Igorot, Kankaney, Kalinga, and Aeta or what traditional academia references as Negrito or pygmy populations, which I believe more properly should be referred to by there traditional name of Ayta or Ati which can be translated to the first people or the ancient people. These two living ancestral population groups were initiates of the gods, Nephilim, Titans and so forth of ancient world mythologies or what science now refers to as our ancient hominid ancestors such as the Denisovans who handed down their knowledge, wisdom, genetics and technology to these chosen survivors from the end of the last world and into the new.

During my time with the Ayta Magbukun whom in the most recent Denisovan genetic study showed that they had the highest level of living Denisovan DNA in the world, I learned some very interesting lessons from my ancestors most importantly that there are many mysteries left in this world to be revealed and places left unexplored. While the narrative may be that the homo Sapien Sapiens is the end all be all and the only hominin left, new discoveries beckon on the horizon and the lack of evidence of something existing does not mean it does not exist only that you are not looking in the correct location. The paradigm shift is upon us and as sleep is wiped from our collective spectacles, might we awaken to a world of creatures who have risen from the abyss, descended from the heavens, and stepped from the shadows? In my conclusion of this multimethod synthesis of all elements and observations available, I reckon we still live in a multispecies hominid world of antediluvian creatures in a state of fervent controlled anticipation to make a comeback.

Presentation: Mysteries of the Igorot Rice Terraces

In my 2022 Philippine exploratory expedition, which began in the deep volcanic jungles of Bataan along the ridgelines of Mt. Mariveles I was searching for the last Denisovans1, A successful endeavor, as I eventually obtained a unanimous vote of acceptance by their wise council of elders, regarding a proposed future long-term ethnography (still awaiting support and logistics), I learned some profound paradigm shattering ethnographic details and Ego-rotting historcities regarding the shared Philippine ancestor. Particularities of the world that we could not fathom with the minds and theologies of modernity. This new perspective obtained, allowed me to ascend into the netherworlds of the unsolved Philippine mysteries within the territories of the legendary Igorot skyworld. After a multi-day pilgrimage we came upon the place of disembarkation, exiting the port of modernity began by traversing a vast gorge by some Indio-genius bridge of Manila Hemp and then ascending the windy ancient mountain-top stone paved stairway, into another realm.

Once you crossed that ancient bridge their was no wifi, no cell signal, and no promise of return. At this moment I looked to my left and looked to my right, it was like I had entered another time, another place, surrounded by clouds (not fog) sky clouds and through the haze you could make out thousands of Mountain pyramids in the backdrop. I knew this was something of a unfathomable magnitude, a mystery of some grandiose antiquity and just then it was as if the mountain stone terraces called out, singing the ancient hymn of the hudhud, which echoed eternally amongst this sacred space, immediately and without compunction the Ego was shattered, the barriers of all things we thought to be true and self evident, shattered and the walls of modernities imaginary lines and placenames came crumbling down.

The smell of panikpikan rushed to reach the nostril, a most delicious traditional smoked native chicken and etag herbal soup, accompanied by the sound of the tak-tak ceremony performed by a living sacred ancestor and culture hero Apo Whang-od, the last mambabatok (sacred tattoo artist). My senses were overwhelmed with amazement, I knew this was a life changing moment, which could never be undone. To sit amongst the clouds with Apo Whang-od, sky pyramids as the backdrop, I gazed into her eyes as she embraced me and it was as if they held all the mysteries of the universe. Eyes they say are the window to the soul and hers were unbound from the quantum entanglements of even Einsteins relativity. A cosmic message relayed through the wire, loud and clear, the ancestors wanted their story told... This is my course, an unveiling, the revelations from the Igorot pyramids of the skyworld.

In the presentation of my research, I synthesize years spent in the field and many long nights sprawled amongst archives to provide Revelations regarding another long debated and squabbled about unsolved mystery of the Philippine archipelago. This is the unraveling of the secret of the Philippine rice terraces. It is my pleasure to clearly state that the preponderance of evidence exhibits without doubt, that the Igorot rice terraces of Northern Luzon and adjacent high mountain agricultural terraces of the surrounding regions and cultures such as those found in Taiwan, China, Thailand, Japan, Vietnam, Indonesia, throughout the Pacific, and reaching into the first settler coastal communities of the Americas were initially developed in Northern Luzon as a multilayered Indigenous response to global climate change, sea-level rise, cosmic triggers, and abrupt deluge events during the Pleistocene epoch (2.5 million -12,000 years ago). This ancient multi-faceted technology was manifested by a multi-species ancestral hominid civilization situated in the Asia/Sunda-Pacific region prior to the arrival of the modern human in SE Asia.
